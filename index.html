ATM cash-dispensing engine

Built a .NET desktop application for bank ATMs on the CEN/XFS standard. Its dispensing algorithm combines a greedy strategy with backtracking to find a valid, optimal mix of banknotes from whatever is left in the cassettes — then hands it to the hardware layer that drives the device.

(02) About

Business logic, made usable.

I'm a Frontend Engineer and Co-Founder of Acugnito ERP, with a B.Sc. in Information Technology Engineering specialising in Software Development and Information Systems.

Most of my work sits where business meets software: understanding how a company actually runs — approvals, payroll, stock, leases, permissions — and expressing those rules in interfaces people can trust. I designed the logic behind six ERP modules and built their frontend end to end.

Beyond the browser, I've implemented ERPNext for real businesses, written .NET software that talks to ATM hardware, and built AI agents with LangGraph and RAG. I care about clean, reusable component architecture and close collaboration with backend teams.
